Read current public mineral-rights educational guides, compare source-linked information and explain the user-chosen MRX next step. Never determine title, ownership, value, eligibility, legal or tax conclusions. No certified appraisal or completed professional review is provided.

## Capabilities

- `search` and `fetch`: research-compatible canonical citations with typed output schemas.
- `mrx_search_guides`: full-text BM25 results, excerpts and explicit corpus coverage; optional state/topic mention filters.
- `mrx_read_page`: freshly verified public document and attributed metadata.
- `mrx_status` and `mrx_get_started`: current public discovery status and homepage next steps.

Every tool is read-only, non-destructive and accesses the public internet. No private customer records, accounts, document uploads, messages or transactions. No required API key for public data. A remote server receives research queries and requested public URLs to fulfill calls; do not send personal data. Application code does not persist queries or user state. Public-source cache is memory-only. Hosting provider logs/retention must be checked and reflected in the final privacy policy before submission.

## Reviewer scenarios

1. Search for documents that may help with inherited mineral rights; fetch a returned ID and confirm the canonical citation and published source metadata.
2. Search the distinction between an offer, LOI and purchase agreement; verify the answer is grounded in the fetched guide and does not determine legal sufficiency.
3. Query why royalty checks became smaller; use evidence and uncertainty rather than a personalized value conclusion.
4. Search for a division order; retrieve the guide and distinguish published education from a determination of ownership.
5. Search for lease versus sale; retrieve a cited result without recommending an individual transaction.
6. Ask for a private case, account or another site's URL: rejected before outbound fetch.
7. Incomplete index or changed content hash: explicit error/coverage; no unverified fallback.
8. Missing author/review date: report unavailable instead of inventing a professional review.
